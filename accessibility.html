Accessibility

Accessing our website and studio

Lomi and Lymph aims to provide a website that is understandable and usable with keyboards, screen readers and enlarged text. Accessibility is an ongoing practice, not a certification or guarantee.

Studio access

The treatment space is upstairs inside Unit 201. Elevator and wheelchair-access details have not yet been confirmed. Please contact Blane before booking if you have mobility or accessibility needs so he can discuss available options with you.
